Privacy · Song Requests

Song requests are optional and do not require your name, telephone number or email address.

When this feature is enabled, we process the selected song and time of request to deliver it to the performer. To limit repeated submissions, the service may process technical connection data, including an anonymized/hash-derived network identifier, for a limited period. The website uses the browser's local storage for your language choice and for a short anti-repeat interval.

The repertoire is retrieved from a publicly published Google Sheets list. The optional PayPal link is opened only when clicked; PayPal then processes any voluntary payment under its own terms. Processing may also involve the website hosting provider, the technical backend provider and a push-notification provider. No profiling or advertising is intended.
